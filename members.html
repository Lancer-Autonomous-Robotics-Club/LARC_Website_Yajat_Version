Membership — practical steps.

  1. Eligibility: any UWindsor undergraduate or graduate student. At least two-thirds of members are undergraduates.
  2. Term: September 1 – August 31. Voting rights at general and election meetings.
  3. Commitment: monthly general meeting + weekly subteam standup.
  4. Start: send the form with your resume. We place you with a subteam within one week.
